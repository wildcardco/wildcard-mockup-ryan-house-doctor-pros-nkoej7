Drafty windows

The fix

We find where the air is getting in, whether that's the sash, the frame, or the wall around it, and reseal, repair, or replace so the heat stays inside.

Storm-damaged siding

The fix

The fix

Leaky roof

The fix

We trace the leak to its true source, which is often flashing or decking rather than the stain on the ceiling, and repair the roof and any hidden water damage.
